Cookie Run Timeline

Playable games are here, but there are also discontinued games here! Explore it all! In chronological order, of course.

OvenBreak
2009
The first game in the franchise! Somehow, we found a game that beat Classic! (Complimentary Skittles!)
OvenBreak 2
2012
A sequel so required, it was practically just an update to OvenBreak! Plus, what kind of Cookie would prefer Skittles over Bear Jellies?
Cookie Run for Kakao
2013
Isn't it great? Korea has a new Cookie Run Game for Kakao! Many things are similar from the last game, but it's still wonderful!
LINE Cookie Run
January 2014
Wow! Cookie Run for Kakao but global! G-good job, Devsisters!
Cookie Run: Munjil Munjil
April 2014
Home to the Cookie heads! Finally, a new type of gameplay! Oh, it's discontinued? Oh...
QQ Cookie Run
May 2014
The side-scrolling gameplay is back...! Now available in China! Nevermind, it's gone.
Cookie Run: OvenBreak
2016
Cookie Run: OvenBreak! Although the same gameplay as almost all the previous games, they curently have the most Cookies of any Cookie Run game!
CookieWars
2018
Defend your tower... I think! I'm not able to find much info about it.
Cookie Run: Puzzle World
2020
The Puzzle gameplay is back, and technically discontinued, but still fully functional!
Cookie Run: Kingdom
Global: 2021
Does Dark Enchantress Cookie think she got all the valuable Cookies? She still has the Ancients left!
Cookie Run: The Darkest Night
November 2023
You'll think Wizard Cookie snuck into your home playing this game! How wonderful!
Cookie Run: Kingdom
China: December 2023
Does Dark Enchantress Cookie think she got all the valuable Cookies? She still has the Ancients left, and two (so far) of the Divine Beasts have awakened!
Cookie Run: Witch's Castle Blast
March 2024
Pop Jellies and hear about the Witches! The ones that are Cookies, of course!
Cookie Run: Tower of Adventures
June 2024
The Cookie with the Shining Heart has come to save the Cookies in the tower! (I don't know what else to write :))
Cookie Run: India
December 2024
Yet another version of Cookie Run for Kakao for a specific place! When can we have one global again? The LINE version was discontinued!
Cookie Run: OvenSmash
March 2026
A multiplayer game? YAS! Now my friend and I can defeat eachother with Cookies in 3D! (Toll House Cookies work too, they just crumble easier.)
Cookie Run: Classic
June 2026
YARRRRRRRRRRRRRRR! JUST WHAT I WAS WISHING FORRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR! Cookie Run for Kakao is now (technically) available globally!
Cookie Run: Crumble
July 2026
Even though this game has Cookies with a mind of their own, it's the baby of the group! Please be kind to it, because it doesn't have the smallest database! *cough cough* OVENSMASH *cough cough*
